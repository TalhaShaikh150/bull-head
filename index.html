"This book is not written for the “If only Ida’s” of the world."

You know the type. “If only Ida done this or if only Ida done that, my life would have been so much better.”

This book is written to help those that are ready and willing to create their own successes now, rather than waiting until it’s too late.

Having spent my younger years working within family-run businesses, I’ve acquired many important personality traits over the course of my life (sometimes the hard way) such as honesty, commitment, respect, empathy, and personal accountability.

This book is a living writing. Lessons that meant nothing the first time through will leap off the pages at other times in your life.
