About the Author

Dale C. Strobach

You can find me in West Central WI keeping it real with my gorgeous wife who has stood by my side for 38+ years, my two enterprising sons, and a few muscle cars in my garage.

I was born a long time ago in North Central WI at a very young age.

Having spent my younger years working within family-run businesses including a dairy farm and a few smaller grocery stores, I’m no stranger to a strong work ethic, and have carried it into other professions over the years.

I’ve acquired many other important personality traits over the course of my life (sometimes the hard way) such as honesty, commitment, respect, empathy, and personal accountability.

"But the most important thing (and the reason for this book existing) is a willingness to serve others."

The goal of this writing is to help you advance toward your own success. If even one topic helps you to do that, the book’s original intent has been met.
