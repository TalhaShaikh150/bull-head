Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/8/2026

This privacy policy describes how Bullheaded Butch Press LLC handles information on bullheadedforsuccess.com (the “Site”), which promotes the book Bullheaded For Success.

Information we collect

The Site is primarily informational. We do not require an account and do not sell books directly on the Site.

  • Information you give us: If you email us or use a contact form, we receive whatever you send (such as your name, email address, and message).
  • Automatically collected information: Like most websites, our hosting or analytics provider may collect technical data such as browser type, device type, general location (country or region), pages visited, and referring site. This is typically collected via cookies or similar technologies.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, payment details, or information from children under 13.

How we use information

We use limited information to:

  • Operate and improve the Site
  • Respond to messages you send us
  • Understand aggregate traffic (for example, which pages are visited)

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The Site may use essential cookies and optional analytics cookies (for example, from a service such as Google Analytics or a privacy-focused alternative). You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how the Site works.

Third-party links

Links to buy the book (including Amazon and other retailers) take you to external sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for how they collect or use information once you leave the Site.

Sharing

We do not share personal information except:

  • With service providers who host the Site or provide analytics, under their own terms
  • If required by law or to protect our rights

Data retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and for ordinary record-keeping. Analytics data is retained according to the provider’s settings, which we aim to keep limited.

Your choices

You may request access to or deletion of personal information you have sent us by contacting us at contact@bullheadedforsuccess.com. Browser settings let you limit cookies.

Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
